# Braidry braids

Show the person this list as it stands, then ask which they want. To start one, follow its command's skill.

- **Literature review** (`/braidry:review`): A full review written from the full texts, every quote and claim checked. Time not yet measured: our estimate is 45 to 90 minutes (rapid: 20 to 40).
- **Check my draft** (`/braidry:check-draft`): Checks your draft's references, quotes and numbers against the sources it cites, with a card per finding, and fixes the ones you accept in a corrected copy.
- **Check a client report** (`/braidry:check-client-report`): Check my draft held to a client standard: every number must trace to a source it cites, and your glossary's banned terms are found. A card per finding; fixes the ones you accept.
- **Sample size and power** (`/braidry:sample-size`): How many people to recruit for a planned comparison or model: completers and recruitment after dropout computed in code from the inputs you state, every number rechecked.
